Return & Refund Policy

We accept return or exchange requests within 5 days of receiving your order.

Products must be unused, unwashed, and returned with original tags and packaging intact.

Refunds are processed only after the returned item passes quality inspection.

Shipping charges may be deducted during refund processing.

Products damaged due to misuse, washing, or missing tags are not eligible for return or exchange.

Refunds are typically processed within 5–7 business days after product verification.
